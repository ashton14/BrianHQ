I built you a website. Not a real one — a command center. One page with everything you check anyway, except it looks like a 1987 arcade cabinet and it talks back to you.

MARKET WATCH
S&P, Nasdaq and the Dow, live, refreshing every minute. A green bull shows up when it's a good day and a red bear when it isn't. E*TRADE is one click away, for better or worse.
SPORTSDESK

BACKYARD GOLF  +  CORNHOLE
